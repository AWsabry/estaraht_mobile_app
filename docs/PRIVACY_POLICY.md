Estaraht ("استرحت", "we", "us", or "our") operates the Estaraht patient app and Est Therapist (استرحت للمعالج) doctor app (together, the "Apps"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your information when you use our Apps and related services.

By using the Apps, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information You Provide

- **Account information:** Name, email address, phone number, age, gender, and password when you register.
- **Profile information:** Profile photo, bio, specializations (for therapists), and medical or professional details you choose to add.
- **Appointment and session data:** Booking details, session notes, and communications related to consultations.
- **Payment information:** Payment and billing details are processed by our payment partners; we do not store full card numbers on our servers.
- **Communications:** Messages you send to support or within the Apps (e.g. chat with your therapist).

### 1.2 Information Collected Automatically

- **Device information:** Device type, operating system, unique identifiers, and app version.
- **Usage data:** How you use the Apps (e.g. features used, time spent) to improve our services and fix issues.
- **Log data:** IP address, access times, and similar technical logs.

### 1.3 Permissions

The Apps may request access to:

- **Camera and microphone:** For video and audio consultations.
- **Notifications:** To send appointment reminders and important updates.
- **Storage:** To save documents, images, or files you choose to upload.

You can deny permissions, but some features (e.g. video calls) may not work.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Create and manage your account and profile.
- Provide therapy/consultation services, including scheduling and video sessions.
- Process payments and prevent fraud.
- Send appointment reminders, updates, and (with your consent) marketing.
- Improve the Apps, fix bugs, and develop new features.
- Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.
- Protect the safety and security of users and our services.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

---

## 3. Sharing of Information

We may share your information only in these situations:

- **Service providers:** With partners who help us run the Apps (e.g. hosting, analytics, payment processing), under strict confidentiality and data protection agreements.
- **Therapists/patients:** As needed to provide consultations (e.g. your therapist sees your appointment and session-related data).
- **Legal requirements:** When required by law, court order, or to protect rights, safety, or property.
- **With your consent:** In any other case, only with your clear consent.

We do not share your personal information with advertisers or for their marketing.

---

## 4. Data Retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you services, comply with law, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time (see "Your Rights" and "Delete Account" below).

---

## 5. Security

We use industry-standard measures (e.g. encryption, access controls, secure connections) to protect your data. No system is completely secure; we encourage you to use a strong password and keep your login details private.

---

## 6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

- **Access** the personal data we hold about you.
- **Correct** inaccurate or incomplete data.
- **Delete** your account and associated data (see "Delete Account" section).
- **Object or restrict** certain processing of your data.
- **Data portability** (receive your data in a portable format where applicable).
- **Withdraw consent** where we rely on consent for processing.
- **Lodge a complaint** with a supervisory authority.

To exercise these rights, contact us at **support@estaraht.com**.

---

## 7. Delete Account

You may permanently delete your account and request deletion of your personal data:

- **In the app:** Open **Settings (More)** → **Delete account**, then follow the steps and confirm with your password. This will delete your account and associated data from our systems in line with our retention and legal obligations.
- **By email:** Send a deletion request to **support@estaraht.com** from the email linked to your account. We will process it within a reasonable time and confirm when done.

For more details, see our [Delete Account](https://estaraht.com/delete-account) page.
