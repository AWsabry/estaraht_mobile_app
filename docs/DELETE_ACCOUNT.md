# Delete Your Account — Estaraht (استرحت)

**Last updated:** [Add date]

You can permanently delete your Estaraht (استرحت) or Est Therapist (استرحت للمعالج) account and request deletion of your personal data at any time.

---

## What Happens When You Delete Your Account

- Your **account** (login and profile) will be permanently removed.
- Your **personal data** (name, email, profile, appointments, session-related data, etc.) will be deleted from our systems in line with our [Privacy Policy](https://estaraht.com/privacy-policy) and legal obligations.
- You will **no longer** be able to sign in or use the app with that account.
- **Appointment and session history** tied to your account will be removed or anonymized as required by law.

This action **cannot be undone**. Please download or save any information you need before deleting your account.

---

## How to Delete Your Account

### Option 1: From the App (fastest)

1. Open the **Estaraht** (patient) or **Est Therapist** (doctor) app.
2. Go to **Settings** (or **More** / الإعدادات).
3. Tap **Delete account** (حذف الحساب / Supprimer le compte).
4. Read the warning and enter your **password** to confirm.
5. Tap **Delete account** again to confirm.

Your account and associated data will be deleted in line with our data retention and legal requirements.

### Option 2: By Email

If you cannot use the app or prefer to request deletion by email:

1. Send an email to **support@estaraht.com** from the **email address linked to your account**.
2. Use the subject line: **"Delete my account"** (or "حذف حسابي" / "Supprimer mon compte").
3. In the message, clearly state that you want to **permanently delete your account and associated data**.
4. We may reply to confirm your identity (e.g. by verifying your email). Once verified, we will process your request and confirm when the deletion is complete.

We will process email requests within a reasonable time (typically within 30 days, unless a longer period is required by law).

## Data We May Retain

For legal, regulatory, or legitimate business reasons, we may retain certain data (e.g. anonymized or aggregated data, or records we are required to keep by law) after your account is deleted. Such data will no longer identify you personally.
